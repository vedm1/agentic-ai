# Protein-Rich Weight Loss Friendly Dessert

## Greek Yogurt Protein Berry Parfait

### Ingredients:
- 1 cup non-fat Greek yogurt (high in protein)
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries)
- 1 tbsp chia seeds or flaxseeds
- 1 scoop vanilla or unflavored protein powder (optional)
- A drizzle of honey or stevia to taste (optional)
- A few chopped nuts for crunch (almonds or walnuts)

### Instructions:
1. In a bowl, mix the Greek yogurt with protein powder until smooth.
2. Layer half of the yogurt mixture in a glass or bowl.
3. Add a layer of mixed berries and sprinkle with chia seeds or flaxseeds.
4. Repeat with the remaining yogurt mixture.
5. Top with a drizzle of honey or stevia and chopped nuts if using.
6. Serve immediately or refrigerate for 30 minutes for a chilled dessert.

---

This dessert is high in protein, low in sugar, and contains fiber-rich berries and healthy fats from nuts and seeds, making it suitable for weight loss.
