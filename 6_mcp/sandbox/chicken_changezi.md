# Chicken Changezi Recipe

## Ingredients
- 500 grams chicken (cut into pieces)
- 2 onions (finely chopped)
- 2 tomatoes (pureed)
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
- 1/4 cup cream
- 1/4 cup milk
- 3-4 green chilies (slit)
- 1/2 cup cashew nuts (soaked in warm water and ground to a paste)
- 2 tablespoons butter or ghee
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

## Instructions
1. Heat butter or ghee in a pan. Add the chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, sauté for a minute.
3. Add chicken pieces and cook until they turn white.
4. Mix in turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook for 5 minutes.
5. Add tomato puree, yogurt, and cashew nut paste. Cook until chicken is tender and the oil starts to separate from the gravy.
6. Stir in cream and milk. Cook on low flame for another 5 minutes.
7. Sprinkle garam masala powder and mix well.
8. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
9. Serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.

Enjoy your delicious Chicken Changezi!
